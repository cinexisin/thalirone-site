**For:** Codex · **From:** Claude Code, for the owner of Thalir Innovations · **Date:** 24 Sep 2026

Read `AGENTS.md` first; its hard rules apply to everything below.

---

## 1. The job

thalirone.com went live on 24 Sep 2026 as a clean, correct, but plain first version. The owner wants the
**UI modernized**: it should look and feel like a confident, current technology company, the kind of
site a homeowner, an architect or a business owner trusts at first glance, while staying fast, accessible
and honest.

You own the **front end**: layout, visual design, components, motion, responsiveness, accessibility and
performance. The **words, facts and prices stay as they are** unless the owner approves a change (see §5).

### Who the site is for
- Homeowners building or renovating (villas and apartments) in **Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh and
  Telangana** (always written in that order), plus architects, builders and interior designers.
- Owners of existing home theatres (Cinema Revival).
- Small and medium Indian businesses that sell on WhatsApp (EKANI).

### The one thing visitors must remember
**"I can run my whole home by messaging it on WhatsApp or Telegram."** That is the company's lead selling
point. Make it the most memorable moment on the site.

### What success looks like
A visitor on a phone understands within five seconds what Thalir does, sees the WhatsApp-control idea
come alive, and taps a WhatsApp button. Every tap is a lead: the business runs on WhatsApp enquiries.

### Known weaknesses to fix
- Section rhythm is repetitive (many same-weight bands and bordered cards); weak visual hierarchy below the hero.
- The home hero's overlapping plates (floor plan + EKANI flow) look busy and don't say "message your home".
- The mobile menu is a bare `<details>`: no transition, no focus handling, doesn't close after navigation.
- No persistent WhatsApp action on mobile; the main conversion depends on scrolling to a button.
- Category pages are long with no in-page navigation.
- The EKANI module list is plain; pricing lacks the clarity of a real pricing section.
- Fonts come from Google Fonts (a render-blocking third-party request).
- Logos are PNG only (no WebP/AVIF, no `srcset`).

## 3. What to build (in priority order)

1. **Hero that shows the idea.** On `/` make the WhatsApp/Telegram conversation the hero visual: messages
   appear one by one (typing indicator, then bubble), a light switches in a small drawn room or the floor plan as
   the reply arrives. Must start from a **fully visible resting state** (nothing hidden at load waiting for an
   observer), run once, respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, and read correctly with JavaScript off.
2. **Sticky mobile action bar** (≤ 768 px): one prominent "WhatsApp us" button (prefilled general enquiry)
   plus a secondary action relevant to the page (site survey / revival consultation / EKANI demo from
   config). It must not cover content or the footer, and must sit clear of the phone's safe-area insets.
   Do **not** add a phone-call button (the number is WhatsApp-only).
3. **Header and navigation:** refined desktop header; mobile menu with a smooth open/close, focus trap,
   Escape to close, closes on link tap, and `aria-expanded`. Works without JS (fallback may stay `<details>`).
4. **Section design system:** a clearer rhythm (hero, proof, detail, action), fewer uniform borders, better
   typographic scale (fluid type with `clamp`), consistent spacing tokens, and one or two stronger "moment"
   sections per page instead of many equal cards.
5. **USP / capability section:** make "Everything you can do from a chat" scannable and attractive (icons per
   group, chat-bubble motifs, possibly tabs on mobile). Keep every capability line and the footnote verbatim.
6. **Category pages:** an in-page sub-nav (Features · How it works · FAQ) that sticks under the header;
   polished process timeline; better FAQ accordions (animated, accessible).
7. **EKANI page:** a proper pricing presentation: module cards with icons and price, a highlighted
   "EKANI One" card with the live "from" price, and "Prices as listed on ekanicrm.com/pricing". **Prices
   must keep coming from the `pricing` object** (`ekaniPricing()` in `build.mjs`); never hard-code them.
8. **Illustrations:** refine the existing drawings for crispness and consistency (stroke weights, labels,
   spacing); light, optional animation is welcome. They stay drawings, and "Example" labels stay.
9. **Performance:** self-host Montserrat and Nunito Sans as subset `woff2` with `font-display: swap`
   (then update the privacy page sentence about Google Fonts); WebP/AVIF logo variants with `srcset` and
   explicit `width`/`height`; no render-blocking third parties.
10. **Contact band and composer:** a friendlier form (inline validation, clear success hand-off to WhatsApp,
   large tap targets). It still only composes a `wa.me` message on the device; no network submission.
11. **Footer, 404 and privacy** brought up to the same standard.

Design freedom: within the palette, logo and rules above, the look is yours. Avoid templated "AI" looks
(purple gradients, glassmorphism everywhere, emoji as section markers, everything centred).

## 4. Constraints

- **Static only.** Output stays plain HTML/CSS/JS in `docs/`, served by GitHub Pages. No server, no SSR.
- **No new dependencies by default.** If you believe a build step or library is essential, justify it in the PR;
  zero-dependency is preferred. Any client JS must be small (target ≤ 15 KB gzipped total, no frameworks).
- **Works without JavaScript**: every page readable, every WhatsApp link clickable, nav usable.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA. Visible focus, keyboard navigation, 44 × 44 px tap targets, contrast ≥ 4.5:1
  for text, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured, semantic landmarks and headings, alt text for meaningful images.
- **Responsive:** no horizontal scroll at 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1440 px.
  *Tip:* headless Chrome won't render narrower than ~500 px; check phone widths in a real browser or with device emulation.
- **Performance budget (mobile Lighthouse):** Performance ≥ 95, Accessibility 100, Best Practices ≥ 95,
  SEO 100 on `/` and `/smart-home-cinema/`. Page weight ≤ 350 KB transferred excluding fonts; CLS < 0.05.
- **SEO kept:** titles, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph tags, JSON-LD (`Organization` with
  `areaServed`), `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`.
- **Theme:** light only (see AGENTS.md).

## 5. Content rules

The site's words live in `src/config.mjs` and were checked line by line against what the business can
prove. Don't rewrite them. Moving, splitting or styling text is fine; changing what it claims is not.
If a design needs new text (a button label, a short heading), keep it within these rules and list every new
or changed string in the PR under **"Copy changes"**.

**May say**
- KNX smart homes designed and programmed in ETS6: lighting (incl. dimming, rooms, whole house),
  air-conditioning (incl. VRV via KNX gateways), curtains and blinds, scenes and schedules.
- Chat control on **WhatsApp or Telegram** in plain English ("living room lights on", "ac cool 22",
  "on geyser for 30m", "which lights are on", "all off", "2 on"); replies confirm; photo alerts when the
  gate opens or the doorbell rings; per-person, per-device permissions, allowed hours, time-limited guest
  invite codes; runs on a Home Assistant controller inside the home.
- Capabilities depend on each home's equipment and are confirmed on the site survey.
- Home cinema: seating and speaker layout incl. Dolby Atmos, installation, measurement and calibration with a
  calibrated microphone and REW (levels, distances, crossovers).
- Cinema Revival: consultation, enhancement, advanced calibration scoped to the system, help finding and fixing
  causes of unwanted smells and eye irritation **within the room** (never medical).
- EKANI: the features and prices already in config/live pricing; "Prices as listed on ekanicrm.com/pricing".
- Process: site survey → written quote → installation & programming → handover.

**Must not say or imply**
- Prices or packages for smart homes or cinema; free visits; trials; response-time promises.
- Customer counts, years in business, "leading", testimonials, awards, certifications or "authorised partner".
- Door locks, "is the door open?"-style answers, sensor readings or energy savings over chat.
- Chat in Tamil, Kannada, Hindi or other languages, free-form AI requests or voice notes as available
  today (the site says they're "on the way").
- Automatic gate opening when a car arrives; "works fully offline"; app-store apps.
- Medical claims; calibration graphs, accuracy figures or reports.
- For EKANI: app-store availability, "data stays in India", security certifications, customer counts.
