Checked 25 September 2026 for the architectural design refresh. This is a record of public-source research, not a claim that the authenticated product interface was inspected.

The web search/open service could not retrieve these pages. Direct HTTPS requests to the public official URLs returned HTTP 200, and their delivered HTML and image references were inspected instead. No credentials, login, private repository, customer record, or authenticated interface was accessed.

The current website repository contains the existing EKANI workflow drawing and labelled concept photography, but no authentic EKANI application screenshot.

## Design decision

Do not substitute a photograph of people or a screenshot of an illustrative marketing diagram and label it as the EKANI product. For this refresh, a restrained HTML workflow demonstration can communicate the existing approved enquiry → lead → GST quote → invoice → recorded payment sequence. Keep its “Example” label visible and accessible. Do not imitate an unverified application interface or add new feature claims from the public marketing site without separate review.

No product image files were imported, and no actual product screenshot was fabricated.

## Future authentic asset

Ask the owner for a current, sanitised capture from a dedicated demo account showing an approved EKANI workflow. Remove personal contact details, customer records, access tokens, and other private information before providing it. Record the capture date, represented module and source. Use actual capture dimensions and responsive compressed variants, keeping small interface text out of images whenever HTML can present it more accessibly.

Until such a capture is available, direct visitors to the existing EKANI demo action and official product links.
